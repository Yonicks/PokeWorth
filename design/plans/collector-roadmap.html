PokeWorth Collector Roadmap

This plan covers the portfolio, card detail, search and overview screens from the five Collectr screenshots you shared, rebuilt in PokeWorth's own navy-and-gold style. For each feature it says where the data would come from, whether that data is free, and which phase builds it.

Scope

What we copy, and what stays ours

Copy

  • Screen layouts and the order of information
  • Feature set: portfolio, price history, per-variant prices, quantity steppers, graded section
  • Interaction patterns: range pills, Raw / Graded tabs, 2-column card grid

Keep from PokeWorth

  • Navy backgrounds, gold accents and price colour from design/home-spec.md
  • Scan-first flow: the camera stays the main action
  • Every price shows provider, variant, currency and date (AC-P2.5)

Leave out

  • Collectr's name, logo, icons and wording
  • Shop, Social, ad banners, promo codes, "Pro" paywall badges
  • Any number we cannot source, such as PSA population or gem rate

Data

Where each number comes from

Data is the hard part. Collectr's charts and graded tables run on years of price history and paid data feeds. PokeWorth runs on TCGdex, which gives today's prices only. Each row is a source we could use and what it gives us.

Order

Build order

Each step ships something you can use on your phone. Steps 1–4 slot into the phases already in docs/dev-phases.md; steps 5 and 6 are new phases.

  1. Tabs and tokens · P3 built

    Five-slot tab bar: Home, Search, a centre Scan button, Portfolio, Profile. Shop and Social are dropped. Theme tokens come from the home spec, and Scan, Confirm and Card detail open above the tabs.

    Every later screen sits inside these tabs.

  2. Card detail · P2 built

    Hero artwork, title block, a price for each printing with provider, currency and date, 7- and 30-day change, the sold-listings link, and a variant picker before any price.

    This is the screen a scan lands on, so it matters most.

  3. Search and sets · P2.4

    Search tab with the camera button, a result grid, and the Show Sets browser.

    Reuses the matcher that was just improved.

  4. Portfolio · P5

    Storage v1 with several portfolios, quantity steppers on Card detail and +1 in Search, the grid, totals, search, filter, bulk actions and CSV export. Most valuable cards appear on Home.

    Once you own cards in the app, the history and overview features have something to show.

  5. History and movers · new P7

    Daily on-device snapshots of portfolio and per-card prices, the price chart with range pills, the Home value chart, market movers for your own cards, and the USD/EUR switch.

    Charts look empty for the first weeks, so this comes after Portfolio.

  6. Graded cards · new P8

    "Add a graded card" with grading company, grade, certificate number and a value you enter yourself. Graded market prices and population only arrive if decision 2 finds a source.

    Collectors can record graded cards now, without data we don't have.

Decisions

Decisions

You accepted all four recommendations on 7 Nov 2026. They are recorded in docs/status.md.

1 · Price history source

On-device snapshots are free and private, but they only cover cards you own and start empty. A scheduled job on Supabase's free tier covers more cards, but adds a server to maintain.

Recommendation: start on-device in P7, and add the server job later only if the charts feel too thin.

2 · Graded prices

No free source shows PSA prices or population inside the app.

Recommendation: manual graded entries plus the sold-listings link for now. Revisit if you'll pay for a data feed.

3 · Tab bar

The screenshots have six tabs: Home, Search, Shop, Social, Portfolio, Profile. P3 planned three or four tabs.

Recommendation: Home, Search, centre Scan, Portfolio, Profile. This also settles P3's open r1 / r2 layout choice.

4 · Several portfolios in the first version

Supporting several portfolios from the start costs little in storage, and adding them later means migrating saved data.

Recommendation: build several portfolios into storage v1 and show one ("Main") until you create another.
